# Requirements

| ID | Name | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-001 | Adjustable illumination | The device shall provide at least two selectable illumination levels. | Draft |
| REQ-002 | Portable power | The device shall operate without a permanent connection to mains power. | Draft |
| REQ-003 | Stable positioning | The device shall remain stable in its intended operating positions. | Draft |
