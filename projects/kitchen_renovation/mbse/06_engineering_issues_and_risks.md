# Engineering Issues and Risks

## Issues

| ID | Issue | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ISSUE-001 | Required quantity of Starlon 3 mm underlay is not yet confirmed. The repair area is approximately 15.68 m². Purchasing strategy remains open: buy one package first and a second if required, or buy two initially. Final quantity depends on actual coverage, cuts and installation waste. | OPEN ISSUE |
| ISSUE-002 | A replacement laminate product has not yet been selected. The retained existing floor is identified by the user as Kaindl Classic Touch 37224, approximately 1382 × 193 × 7–8 mm, without a visible V-groove. Compatibility of its locking profile with currently sold products remains TBD and requires a physical fit test. Decorative match is not required. | OPEN ISSUE |
| ISSUE-003 | Floor-cavity depths of approximately 100 mm and 120 mm have been reported, while DEC-001 selects STEICO flex 036 in 100 mm thickness. Treatment of the approximately 120 mm cavities remains TBD. | OPEN ISSUE |
| ISSUE-004 | Water-management strategy above the upper OSB remains unresolved. ISOCELL AIRSTOP FLEX 50 mm was discussed as an airtight joint-tape option, while a dedicated water-resistant or waterproof layer was discussed only as a proposal. No such system has been selected. | OPEN ISSUE |
| ISSUE-005 | The reconstructed timber floor must accommodate the existing group of service penetrations rising from the floor near the wall, including plumbing/drainage and electrical/conduit routes visible in the current exposed installation area. The final detail is TBD and must address routing, mechanical protection, separation and future inspection/access while also preserving continuity of the floor thermal-insulation layer and restoring the required airtight layer around all penetrations. Exact airtightness plane, sealing products/details and treatment of insulation around each service remain TBD. | OPEN ISSUE |
