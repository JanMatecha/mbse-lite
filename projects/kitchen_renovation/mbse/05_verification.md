# Verification

| ID | Verification | Method | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| VER-001 | Verify mechanical compatibility of a candidate replacement laminate with the retained existing laminate. | Physical test-fit using an original retained board: test long and short edges; confirm the joint closes without forced modification, remains locked, has no visible joint gap, and does not create an unacceptable height mismatch. | PROPOSAL |
| VER-002 | Verify that the installed replacement finished-floor product is laminate flooring as selected for the repaired area. | Product/installation inspection before final acceptance. | PROPOSAL |
