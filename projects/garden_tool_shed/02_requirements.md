# Requirements

| ID | Name | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-001 | Storage capacity | The shed shall accommodate the agreed inventory of garden tools and equipment. | Draft |
| REQ-002 | Weather protection | The shed shall protect stored items from precipitation under normal local weather conditions. | Draft |
| REQ-003 | Access | The shed shall provide access suitable for the agreed stored-item set and intended use. | Draft |
| REQ-004 | Site compatibility | The shed footprint and placement shall fit within the confirmed available site envelope and applicable constraints. | Draft |
| REQ-005 | Ground-screw support | The shed shall be supported on ground screws rather than requiring a poured concrete slab, strip footing or similar permanent concrete foundation. | Confirmed |
| REQ-006 | Relocatability | The shed and its site interface shall allow the structure to be dismantled or released from its support and relocated without demolition of a permanent concrete foundation. | Confirmed |
| REQ-007 | Inventory accommodation | The storage layout shall accommodate long-handled tools, heavy hand tools, small garden tools, flower pots, hoses and a battery lawn mower. | Confirmed |
| REQ-008 | Target footprint | The design shall target an external footprint of approximately 4.0 m × 1.2 m. Final tolerances and exact dimensions are TBD after site and layout confirmation. | Draft |
| REQ-009 | Standing clearance | The interior clear height and access geometry shall allow an adult user to enter and stand upright inside. Exact minimum clear height is TBD. | Draft |
| REQ-010 | Separate mower compartment | The battery lawn mower shall be stored in a dedicated enclosed compartment physically separated from the main storage volume. | Confirmed |
| REQ-011 | Practical organization | The internal arrangement shall provide practical storage appropriate to both long tools and small loose items without preventing normal user access. | Draft |

Exact item dimensions, quantities, footprint tolerance and standing-clearance value remain `TBD` until the first layout and site measurements are available.
