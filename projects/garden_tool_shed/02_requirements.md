# Requirements

| ID | Name | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-001 | Storage capacity | The shed shall accommodate the agreed inventory of garden tools and equipment. | Draft |
| REQ-002 | Weather protection | The shed shall protect stored items from precipitation under normal local weather conditions. | Draft |
| REQ-003 | Access | The shed shall provide access suitable for the agreed stored-item set and intended use. | Draft |
| REQ-004 | Site compatibility | The shed footprint and placement shall fit within the confirmed available site envelope and applicable constraints. | Draft |

Quantitative values and acceptance criteria remain `TBD` until the inventory, site and user expectations are captured.
