## Right-end shelving intent

The shelving unit on the right short end wall is intended mainly for small and medium garden items such as flower pots, seeds, garden gloves, pruning shears, pliers and other small garden tools. The exact contents, shelf depth, number of shelves, shelf spacing and use of bins/boxes remain open and shall be determined from the first layout.

The inventory is sufficient to start layout work, but exact quantities and dimensions remain to be captured where they materially affect the design.
